Levers

A lever is a strong bar that rests on a point called the fulcrum. Push one end down and the other end goes up! Levers help us lift heavy things with less effort.

💪 Your body has levers too!

💪
Lifting with your arm

Your elbow is the fulcrum. Your muscle pulls in the middle. Your hand holds the load. 3rd class lever!

🦶
Standing on tiptoes

Your toes are the fulcrum. Your body weight is in the middle. Your leg muscle pulls up. 2nd class lever!

🙂
Nodding your head

Your neck is the fulcrum in the middle. Your face is on one side and your muscles pull on the other. 1st class lever!

💡 Other ways to make a lever

You don't need LEGO to make a lever! Try these at home or in class.

🧱 More LEGO ideas

🛝
Minifigure seesaw

Put two minifigures on the ends. Move the fulcrum until they balance.

🎯
Catapult

Put a light brick on a short arm. Hit the long arm to throw it. Measure how far it goes!

🛞
Wheelbarrow

Use a wheel as the fulcrum. Put the load in the middle and lift the handles.

🏗️
Crane arm

Build a long arm on a tower. Add a weight at the back so it lifts loads at the front.
