    Touch light

    Tips and hints

    • Only the sensor should touch the water: keep the battery and the IC dry.
    • Water is the "switch": it joins C and D. A button would need a finger, not water.
    • Salt water works better than fresh water, and oil does not work at all.
    • Check the LED's + side: it lights in one direction only.
    • Test one change at a time, then dip it again.
