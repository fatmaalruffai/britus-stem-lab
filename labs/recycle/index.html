    Step 5 · Test: sort by size

    Make the truck sort

    The book truck drops every box in the same place: it does not sort yet. Engineers' idea (WeDo): take off the back plate and cut a hole in the bed. Boxes small enough fall through the hole into the green bin; bigger boxes slide over it into the blue bin.

    Choose a hole. Each run tips one small cube (2×2), one long box (4×2) and one big L-box (4×4). Sizes are in LEGO studs.

    Step 6 · Math and science

    Volume: measure with cubes

    Book: When the materials are sorted they are measured by volume, not weight. Volume is how much space something takes up. Count it in cubic units: volume = length × width × height.

    🧲 Sort by material: cans

    WeDo idea: sort by properties other than shape. Steel cans and aluminium cans are the same size, so a hole cannot sort them. Real recycling centres use a magnet.

    Step 9 · Other designs

    Sort it another way

    Book: Try to create your own design. Recycling centres use many machines to sort. Run each idea in 3D, then design your own.

    Book design

    Tipping truck with a hole

    Your truck: small boxes fall through the hole, big ones slide off.

    Example 1

    Conveyor belt and screen

    A belt carries everything over a screen with holes (a "trommel" does this in a spinning drum). Small things drop through.

    ✔ GoodSorts a steady stream all day.
    ✘ Watch outHoles get blocked; someone must clean them.
    Example 2

    Motion-sensor gate

    WeDo idea: use the Motion Sensor to detect size. Tall boxes are close to the sensor, so a motor turns a gate and sends them to another bin.

    ✔ GoodThe program decides, so you can change the rule.
    ✘ Watch outNeeds a sensor, a motor and careful testing.
    Example 3

    Robot arm

    WeDo idea: a robot arm picks each object and drops it in the right bin.

    ✔ GoodCan sort many different kinds.
    ✘ Watch outSlow: one object at a time.
    Example 4

    Magnet drum

    A spinning magnet drum lifts steel cans away from everything else.

    ✔ GoodFast and simple for steel.
    ✘ Watch outOnly works for magnetic metals.
    Example 5

    Air blower

    A fan blows light paper off the belt into its own bin; heavy glass and cans keep going.

    ✔ GoodSorts by weight with no touching.
    ✘ Watch outWet paper is heavy and may not fly.

    Tips and hints

    • Match the "decider" to the property: holes and sensors for size, magnets for steel, fans for light things.
    • Measure the boxes first, then choose the hole: bigger than the small boxes, smaller than the big ones.
    • Change one thing at a time when you test, like you did in Discover.
    • Give your program time: wait long enough to load, flip long enough to empty.
    • One bin per group: to sort into 3 groups you need 3 bins and 2 deciders.
