    Step 10 · Other designs
    
 
    Build it another way
    
 
    The book asks if you will build the seesaw as shown or improve it. Here are five other ways to show warm periods and ice ages with the same sensor and servo. Watch each one run in 3D, then invent your own.
    
 
    Book design
    Seesaw
    Warm side down in warm periods, ice side down in ice ages.
    
 
    Example 1
    Climate dial
    The servo turns a pointer over a half-circle dial from "ice age" to "warm period", like a big thermometer.
    
  
    ✔ GoodShows how warm, not just warm or cold.
    ✘ Watch outUse map() so the pointer moves smoothly.
    
 
    Example 2
    Growing glacier
    A white "ice sheet" block slides out over the land when it is cold and pulls back when it is warm.
    
  
    ✔ GoodShows what really happens to ice in ice ages.
    ✘ Watch outNeeds a rack-and-gear slider: harder to build.
    
 
    Example 3
    Climate pendulum
    A pendulum swings slowly between a sun and a snowflake, showing that the climate keeps oscillating.
    
  
    ✔ GoodA perfect picture of "oscillation".
    ✘ Watch outHang the weight low so the servo is not overloaded.
    
 
    Example 4
    Sea level tower
    Warm periods melt ice, so the sea rises. A blue "sea" plate moves up a tower in warm periods and down in ice ages.
    
  
    ✔ GoodLinks temperature to sea level rise.
    ✘ Watch outThe cord and pulley must be tight.
    
 
    Example 5
    Tilting Earth
    One cause of ice ages is the slow change in the tilt of Earth's axis. A globe on the servo tilts a little more or less.
    
  
    ✔ GoodShows a cause, not just the result.
    ✘ Watch outThe real change is small (about 22° to 24.5°) and very slow.

    Tips and hints
    
  
      
   
    • The DHT11 is slow: read it every 2 seconds, not faster.
      • 
   
    • Test safely: a hair dryer on low, held 20 cm away. Never wet the sensor.
      • 
   
    • Balance the seesaw before you attach the servo, so the servo does not strain.
      • 
   
    • Label the sides clearly: sun for warm periods, snowflake for ice ages.
      • 
   
    • Use map() for smooth movement, and two thresholds to stop shaking.
