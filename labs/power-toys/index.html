⚡ Introduction

Power Up the Toys

Toys help children grow, have fun and learn at the same time. There are many kinds of toys: science kits, educational toys, stuffed toys, modeling clay and electronic toys. Electronic toys need batteries to work.

❓ Problem
🧑‍💼
Imagine that you work in a toy company. The company asks your team to place the batteries in the toys. Before you decide, try the series and parallel connection of the batteries.
🎯 Challenge: Act as an electrical engineer, who designs or manages electricity systems. Test series and parallel connection of batteries and design an electronic toy. Build your circuit, draw your prototype, and present your solution to your friends.
💡 How electricity works

What is a circuit?

Electricity flows out of the battery, around a path, and back into the battery. This path is called a circuit. It must be a complete loop. Tap the switch to close and open the circuit.

The switch is open. There is a gap, so electricity cannot flow. The bulb is off.

✔️ The circuit must use the batteries as long as possible.

✔️ The circuit must be placed inside the toy in the prototype.

✔️ The way the batteries are connected must be shown in the prototype.

⚠️ Notes of caution

⚠️ Be careful not to short-circuit the battery in the circuit.

What is a short circuit?

A short circuit happens when a wire connects the + and − of a battery directly, with no bulb or motor in between. Try it safely here. Never do it with real batteries!

The bulb is on. The electricity goes through the bulb.
