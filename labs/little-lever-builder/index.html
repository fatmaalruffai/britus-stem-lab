Little Lever Builder

Build levers and use them · Grade 3 homework

Hello, young engineer! 👋

A lever is a strong bar that turns on a point called the fulcrum. Push one end down and the other end goes up! Today you will build 3 levers, test them, and find levers at home.

The 3 parts of a lever

🔺Fulcrum The point the lever turns on. It stays still.
📦Load The heavy thing you want to lift.
✋Effort Your push or pull.

🏠 Mission 4: Levers at home

These things at home are levers! Tap a thing, then tap the box where it belongs.

1️⃣ First class: the Fulcrum is in the middle.
2️⃣ Second class: the Load is in the middle.
3️⃣ Third class: the Effort is in the middle.
