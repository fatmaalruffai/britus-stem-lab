Step 1 · Problem and plan

The safety expert's tool

The problem (book)

An occupational health and safety expert in a factory makes routine checks to avoid accidents and injuries. He or she measures the current of some electric systems to make sure they are safe.

Challenge (book): Act as an electric engineer. Design a circuit that measures the current, put it inside a tool, and show how it measures the electric flow.

    Step 3 · Test it (book)

    Turn it on and read the meter

    Book: 1) Turn on the switch 15. 2) The arrow of the microammeter will be deflected and start moving, indicating the value of the current. A current is measured in Amperes (A).

    How to read the galvanometer

    The scale goes from 0 to 10. On this meter, each number = 50 µA (microamperes). So the full scale (10) is 500 µA. 1 mA = 1000 µA.

    1. Turn on the switch (button under the 3D model). Where does the needle stop?
    2. So the current is how many µA? (number × 50)
    µA
    3. Turn the switch OFF. What happens to the needle, and why?
