The problem (book)

Sheraz and Luqman are planning a surprise party for their friend Rehan, but they don't know when he will arrive. They want a spy robot that gives a light or sound signal when it detects him arriving.

Brainstorm (book): how will the robot know someone is coming?

Build it step by step, like the book model. For each step, find the right part in the box (some are traps!), then answer the question. Every mistake is counted.

    Step 5 · Discover (book)

    Different sounds, loops and light

    Book: Program it with different sounds, apply loop to see how it performs. You can also try light as a signal.

    A · Different sounds

    Change the number in the Play sound block. WeDo 2.0 has sounds 1 to 20.

    B · Apply a loop

    Without a loop the program ends after the first beep. Put the Wait and Sound blocks inside a Repeat, then send Rehan, his sister and his dad in one by one.

    C · Light as a signal

    Swap the sound for the Light block: the Smarthub light changes colour. Which colour number is red?

    Which signal is better for a surprise party, and why?
