Step 1 · Problem and plan

A diaper that tells you it is wet

The problem (book)

A wearable technology company makes baby diapers. They want tiny electric circuits that give an alarm when the diaper is wet, so the wet diaper can be changed quickly and the baby's skin stays healthy.

Challenge (book): act as an electrical engineer and design a baby diaper that gives an alarm when it becomes wet.

    Tips and hints

    • Book rule: the alarm needs light and sound.
    • Wetness is the switch: it joins the touch plate's two parts. A button would need someone to press it.
    • Keep the batteries and IC dry and sealed: only the touch plate gets wet.
    • Small and soft: anything a baby wears must be light and comfortable.
    • Test one change at a time, then drop water again.
