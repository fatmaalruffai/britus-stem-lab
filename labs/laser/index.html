Step 1 · Materials and setup (book)

Build the safe house

A laser security system makes an invisible tripwire with a laser beam. When someone breaks the beam, the alarm goes off. Museums and other secure places use it to catch people who enter without permission.

Arduino Uno R3The brain: runs your code, reads A0 and switches pins 2 and 6.
Laser module (KY-008)650 nm red laser diode on a small black board: pins S, middle, −.
LDR moduleA light dependent resistor on a blue board with VCC, GND, DO and AO pins.
Active buzzerBeeps by itself when its + leg gets 5 V (HIGH).
Breadboard + jumper wiresShare 5 V and GND through the + and − rails.

Choose the right part for each step. Some parts in the tray are not needed.

    Tips
    • Put the beam at knee height: people cannot step over it easily.
    • Shade the LDR from room light and sunlight.
    • Use mirrors to cover a wide area with one laser.
    • Test the threshold in the real room, day and night.
    • Never point the laser at eyes.
