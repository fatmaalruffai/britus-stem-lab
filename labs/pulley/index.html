Step 1 · Meet the pulley

What is a pulley? 🤔

🏫Book: A pulley is a rope wrapped over a wheel to make it easier to lift heavy things. Pulling the rope down makes the load go up!

Try it with the school flag. Press the button and watch the flag.

Words to know (book)

🔍 Pulley hunt

Book: Engineers use pulleys in cranes, elevators, flagpoles, zip lines, bicycle chains, clotheslines, water well buckets, window blinds and boats. Tap every picture that uses a pulley!

Step 10 · Pulleys around us

Pulleys in our town 🏙️

Press each one to see how its pulley works.

At school

🚩 Flagpole

A wheel at the top: you pull down, the flag goes up. The pulley changes the direction of your pull.

In the village

🪣 Water well

Turn the handle and the rope winds up. The bucket of water comes up from deep down.

At the building site

🏗️ Crane

Big cranes use pulleys and strong ropes to lift heavy blocks high up.

At the mall

🛗 Elevator

A pulley at the top. When the elevator goes up, a heavy weight on the other side goes down to help.

At home

🪟 Window blinds

Pull the cord down and the blinds go up, over a little pulley at the top.
