Discussion questions (tap to check your ideas)
  1. What are the parts of a flower? Petals, stamen (filament + anther), pistil (stigma, style, ovary), sepals and nectar.
  2. How do animals help plants reproduce? They carry pollen from one flower to another while they feed on nectar.
  3. What are the names of these processes? Pollination, biotic pollination (by animals), cross-pollination (between two plants) and seed dispersal.
  4. What are three stages in a flowering plant's life? Seed → seedling → mature plant with flowers.
  5. What is the role of a flower? To make seeds so new plants can grow.
  6. Do all flowers need a pollinator? No. Some use wind or water, and some can pollinate themselves.

Introduction: what is pollination?

One interesting step in plant reproduction is moving pollen to the stigma of a flower. This is called pollination. It happens with the help of wind, water and animals (pollinators). Flowers attract animals with their colour, size, smell and nectar.

Vocabulary
WordMeaning
PollenA very fine powder made by trees, flowers, grasses and weeds to fertilize other plants of the same kind.
AntherThe part of a flower that produces pollen.
StigmaThe part of a flower that receives pollen.
PollinatorAn animal that moves pollen from the male anther of a flower to the female stigma of a flower.
Stamen / styleThe stamen is the male part (filament + anther). The style holds up the stigma.
Cross-pollinationPollen moves from one plant to a different plant of the same kind.
Seed dispersalThe ways seeds travel away from the parent plant.
NectarA sweet liquid with sugars, vitamins, salts and oils. It is high-energy food for pollinators.

Build tips
  1. Open WeDo 2.0 → Guided Projects → Science → 5. Plants and Pollinators and follow the steps.
  2. Build a strong base first so the model does not tip over.
  3. Put the motion sensor in the top of the flower so it sees the bee on top of the flower.
  4. Use a transparent brick to show the pollen.
  5. The bee must pass just above the flower without touching it.
The book's program

Start → Motor power 4 → Motor this way → Wait for motion sensor → Motor off → Play sound 2

How the program works
  1. Start runs the program.
  2. Motor power 4 sets the speed (1 is slow, 10 is fast).
  3. Motor this way turns the bee around.
  4. Wait for motion sensor waits until the sensor sees the bee near the flower.
  5. Motor off stops the bee at the flower.
  6. Play sound 2 plays a sound to show the bee is collecting pollen.
STEM careers

Botanical Scientist, Plant Biologist and Ethnobotanist (who studies how people use plants) all study plants and pollination.

Maths: comparing decimals
  1. Line up the decimal points.
  2. Compare digit by digit from the left: ones, tenths, hundredths, thousandths.
  3. 87.071 and 87.058: the tenths are both 0, but the hundredths are 7 and 5, so 87.071 is greater.
Troubleshooting
  • Smarthub will not connect: check the batteries, then press the green button again.
  • Bee does not stop: check that the Wait For block uses the motion sensor, and the sensor faces the bee.
  • Bee stops past the flower: the motor is too fast. Use a lower power.
  • Nothing moves: check the motor cable is plugged into the Smarthub.

9. Use the model: new flowers and pollinators

Now that you have built the bee, think about how you could build a new flower and a pollinator that would be attracted to it. Plan your design, then test it. There are no building instructions for this part: it is your own idea!

Example 1 · New flower + new pollinator

Hummingbird and a red tube flower

Seen from above. The hummingbird is attached to the axle, like the bee. The flower is red and shaped like a long tube, which hummingbirds love. The motion sensor stays inside the flower, and a transparent brick is the pollen.

Program that acts differently: Start → Motor power 7 → Motor that way → Wait for motion sensor → Motor off → Light red → Play sound 5

Example 2 · New scenario

Cross-pollination with a second flower

The bee collects pollen at flower A, then carries it to flower B of the same kind. Now flower B can make seeds.

Tip: the Smarthub has only two ports (motor + motion sensor). To reach flower B, use Motor on for 2 seconds (time) after the first stop, or connect a second Smarthub.
