Build circuits and power up toys

Hello, young engineer! 👋

Toys need batteries to work. Today you will build two circuits with batteries and then use them to power up some toys!

🔗 Mission 1: Batteries in a line

Build the circuit on the snap board. The two batteries are in a line: + touches −.

1Look at the yellow shadow 👀
2Find the part that matches it in the parts bin 🧺
3Drag it onto the shadow 👆
🧺 Parts bin

⚡ It works!

The circuit is a complete loop, so electricity flows. The motor spins and the bulb lights up!

🔋 3 V + 🔋 3 V = ⚡ 6 V

Batteries in a line add up. That gives lots of power!

🪜 Mission 2: Batteries side by side

Now build a circuit where the two batteries are side by side: + with +, and − with −.

1Look at the yellow shadow 👀
2Find the part that matches it in the parts bin 🧺
3Drag it onto the shadow 👆
🧺 Parts bin

⚡ It works!

Both batteries share the work to light the bulb.

🔋 3 V side by side 🔋 = ⚡ 3 V

The voltage stays 3 V, but the batteries last longer! 🕒
