    Step 4 · Code (book steps 1 and 2)
    
 
    Speed dial + power light
    
 
    Book: as the motor speeds up, the LED gets brighter; as it slows down, the LED gets dimmer. The book's sample only switches the LED on above 500. Do better: make the brightness follow the speed. Fill in every ?.
    
 
    
 
    Note: analogWrite() gives "in-between" brightness only on PWM pins (marked ~). If your LED only switches fully on or off, check your controller's pin map and move it to a PWM port.
    
 
    
 
    1. Turn the potentiometer (under the 3D model) to slow: the LED is dim.
    2. Turn it to fast: the LED is bright.
    3. Turn it back to stop: the LED is off.

    Step 10 · Other designs
    
 
    Build it another way
    
 
    The book asks if you will replicate the model or improve it. Here are five other designs, in 3D. Then invent your own.
    
 
    Kit design
    Chain-drive windmill
    Two chains carry the servo's turn up the tower and speed it up.
    
 
    Example 1
    Modern 3-blade turbine
    Three long, thin blades on a tall tower, like the turbines on wind farms.
    
  
    ✔ GoodThe best balance of power and smooth turning.
    ✘ Watch outLong blades must be light and stiff.
    
 
    Example 2
    Direct drive (no chains)
    Put the servo right at the top, on the blade axle. Fewer parts, nothing to slip.
    
  
    ✔ GoodSimple and quiet. Many new turbines are built like this.
    ✘ Watch outThe heavy motor sits high up; the tower must be strong.
    
 
    Example 3
    Vertical turbine (Savonius)
    Two curved scoops turn around an upright axle. It catches wind from any direction.
    
  
    ✔ GoodNo need to face the wind; good on rooftops.
    ✘ Watch outTurns slowly and makes less power.
    
 
    Example 4
    Farm water-pump windmill
    Many blades on a small wheel. Strong turning force at low speed, perfect for pumping water on farms.
    
  
    ✔ GoodStarts turning even in light wind.
    ✘ Watch outToo slow for making much electricity.
    
 
    Example 5
    Water wheel
    Same idea, different renewable: moving water pushes paddles around.
    
  
    ✔ GoodWater flows day and night, not only when it is windy.
    ✘ Watch outOnly works next to a river or stream.

    Tips and hints
    
  
      
   
    • Balance the blades: the same length and weight on every side, or the tower shakes.
      • 
   
    • Keep chains tight but not too tight, or they jump off the gears.
      • 
   
    • Test the dial first with the blades off, then add them.
      • 
   
    • Show the power: brightness that follows speed is better than on/off.
      • 
   
    • Safety: fingers away from the spinning blades and the chains.
