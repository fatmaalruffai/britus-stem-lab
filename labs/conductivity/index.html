    Step 3 · Water solution

    Prepare the water

    When salt dissolves in water, it breaks into tiny charged particles called ions. Ions carry electricity through the water.

    1. Fill two cups with tap water.
    2. Add salt to cup 1.
    3. Stir it to dissolve the salt.
    Start by filling both cups with tap water.
